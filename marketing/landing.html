What's inside

Everything your hosts need.
Nothing they don’t.

Three simple ideas, executed without compromise.

Zero double-bookings

A two-layer lock — Redis plus a Postgres unique index — guarantees the same table can’t be reserved twice. Even on a Friday at 19:30.

WhatsApp reminders & waitlist

Automated reminders 24h and 1h before, sent from your registered Fonnte or Wablas number. Cancellations auto-promote the waitlist.

Live floor & walk-ins

One screen for the whole service — color-coded floor, today’s timeline, walk-ins added in two taps. Updates in real time.

Live service

Run a busy Friday from one screen.

Today’s timeline on the left, the floor plan on the right, real-time updates throughout. Your hosts seat, move, and check in guests in two taps — without scrolling, refreshing, or remembering anything.

  • Color-coded reservation states across the entire timeline
  • Walk-ins captured in 3 taps with table suggestions
  • ⌘K to find any guest by name, phone, or confirmation code
  • WebSocket-powered — new bookings appear instantly

WhatsApp built in

The reminder you don’t have to send.

Tamu sends WhatsApp confirmations and reminders from your number. When a guest cancels, the next person on the waitlist is automatically offered the slot — with a 30-minute window to claim it.

  • Templates with merge tags — name, time, party size, occasion
  • Per-restaurant Fonnte / Wablas integration · your sender, your brand
  • Email fallback if WhatsApp fails — no manual chasing
  • Replies route back to the right reservation automatically
